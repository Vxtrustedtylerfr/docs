These policies are mandatory for all Networkerlc HR team members. As a high-ranking member, you are expected to read, understand, and consistently uphold every policy outlined on this page.

<Warning>
  Failure to follow these policies may result in termination from your role.
</Warning>

## Ticket Policies

As a team member, you must follow these rules whenever you are handling tickets:

1. All members of Management are granted access to both General Support and Management Tickets for effective coordination and resolution.
2. You are required to claim your tickets. If a ticket goes unclaimed, any other eligible staff member may take it over.
3. You must request permission before interrupting an active ticket. This requirement does not apply to members holding Directive roles.
4. You are obligated to use proper spelling, punctuation, and grammar (SPaG) in all tickets to maintain professionalism and clarity.
5. Unless you hold the Staff Supervisor (SS) designation, you do not have the authority to handle MGMT tickets.

## Public Relations Policies

If you are a member of the Public Relations (PR) team, the following rules govern how you interact with tickets:

- You may access general support tickets, but only PR leaders are permitted to handle administrative or staff-related tickets.
- If you are personally involved in an active report, you must not attempt to interfere with, influence, or access that ticket in any way without explicit authorization.
- Unauthorized interference will result in an infraction and possible further disciplinary action.

<Warning>
  If you are caught attempting to interfere with, influence, or access an active report ticket involving yourself without authorization, you will be placed on administrative leave pending investigation.
</Warning>

## Promotions

You are permitted to promote any staff member at any time, provided you have received approval from a Management or Senior Management member beforehand.

### Rank Cooldowns & Caps

The table below outlines the promotion cooldown period and the member cap for each rank tier. You must respect these cooldowns between promotions.

| Rank Tier           | Promotion Cooldown | Member Cap       |
|---------------------|--------------------|------------------|
| Moderation Team     | 14 days (all ranks) | No cap           |
| Administration Team | 21 days (all ranks) | 20 members       |
| Staff Supervisor    | 38 days             | 5 members        |
| Management          | 38 days             | 2 members        |

<Note>
  Cooldowns apply regardless of which specific rank within a tier the promotion targets. For example, any promotion within the Moderation Team always carries a 14-day cooldown.
</Note>
