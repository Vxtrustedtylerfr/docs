Each rank within the HR team is assigned specific quota expectations, so it's important that you familiarize yourself with the requirements tied to your role. Meeting your quota consistently demonstrates your commitment to the team and helps keep the server healthy and active.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Management">
    As a member of the **Management** tier, your weekly quota depends on your specific rank:

    - **Management** — You are required to log at least **1 hour of activity per week** and send a minimum of **250 messages per week** in any public chat channel.
    - **Community Management** — You are required to send a minimum of **250 messages per week** in any public chat channel.

    In addition to these activity benchmarks, you are expected to complete all **management tasks** assigned to you by the Executive Team. Detailed descriptions of those tasks can be found in the relevant section of this handbook.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Operations">
    As a member of the **Operations** team, you are not subject to a fixed weekly quota. Instead, your responsibilities are centered on the overall health of the server:

    - Maintaining the server's activity and vitality.
    - Ensuring that Management team members are upholding server values and following the rules.

    Your role is integral to holding the rest of the team accountable and keeping the community environment in good standing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Executives">
    As a member of the **Executive Team**, you are not subject to a fixed weekly quota. Your focus is on high-level oversight and server stewardship:

    - Maintaining the server's overall activity and vitality.
    - Overseeing Management and ensuring they are effectively executing their assigned tasks.

    You set the standard for the rest of the team, and your leadership directly shapes how well the server functions day to day.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  Failure to meet your quota may result in a **Quota Infraction**. Repeated infractions can lead to further disciplinary action, so make sure you are keeping up with your weekly requirements.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  If you have any questions or uncertainties about your quota, don't hesitate to reach out to a fellow HR member — they'll be happy to help clarify your requirements.
</Tip>
