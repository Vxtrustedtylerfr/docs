## What's in this Handbook

This handbook is organized into four core areas to make it easy for you to find what you need for your role.

**Tasks** walks you through the specific duties tied to each HR position. Weekly tasks are assigned by Directors or Senior Management and may range from supervising Discord staff to resolving management tickets. Community Management is responsible for all Discord-related issues, while Staff Supervision oversees staff conduct and quota infractions.

**Quota** outlines what is expected of you on a weekly basis. Management members must meet a minimum of one hour of activity per week, along with 250 messages in public channels. Operations and Executive team members are not held to a fixed quota but are expected to maintain server vitality and ensure management teams are fulfilling their responsibilities.

**Policies** details the rules you are required to follow as a member of the HR team. This includes proper ticket handling procedures, promotion cooldowns and rank caps per tier, and specific guidelines governing Public Relations members. Non-compliance with these policies may result in termination from your role.

**Guides** provides practical walkthroughs for your operational duties, including how to handle support tickets and how to conduct review boards. These guides are designed to ensure every HR member operates consistently and professionally.
