As a High Ranking Member, you are responsible for managing incoming support tickets with accuracy and professionalism. This guide covers the two most common ticket types you will encounter — purchase claims and paid advertisement requests — and outlines exactly how you should handle each one.

## Confirming Purchases

When a user opens a ticket to claim a purchase, you must follow the verification protocol below before taking any action. Skipping steps can lead to unauthorized or mistaken claims being processed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check for a button error">
    If the user reports an error with the **"Claim Purchase"** button, do not attempt to process the claim immediately. Begin by navigating to the **#transactions** channel to verify the purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the purchase in the logs">
    Search the **#transactions** channel for a record of the user's purchase. Confirm that the transaction appears in the logs before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process or pause the claim">
    - **If the purchase is confirmed in the logs**, proceed with processing the claim manually on the user's behalf.
    - **If there is no record of the purchase**, do not proceed. Hold the ticket and inform the user that further verification is required before the claim can be processed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Paid Advertisements

When a user submits a ticket requesting a paid advertisement, you are responsible for reviewing the content before it is approved or denied. Work through the steps below carefully for every request.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the advertisement">
    Open the latest submission in the advertisements channel and examine it thoroughly for any errors or issues before making a decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deny if there are issues">
    If you identify any of the following problems, click **"Deny"** and clearly state the reason for the denial in your response:
    - The submitter appears to be a troll
    - The invite link is invalid or expired
    - The content is inappropriate or violates guidelines
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept if everything looks good">
    If the advertisement meets all requirements and raises no concerns, click **"Accept"** to add the user to the **paid advertisement queue**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you believe a user should not be approved for a paid advertisement for any reason that is not clearly listed in the guidelines, **do not deny or approve on your own.** Bring the case to the High Ranking Team and discuss it before taking any action.
</Note>
