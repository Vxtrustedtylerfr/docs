As a High Ranking Member, you may be tasked with organizing and running a Staff Review Board. This is a formal process that requires careful scheduling, a properly composed panel of members, and thorough preparation before the review begins. Follow each step below to ensure the board is conducted correctly and fairly.

## Step 1 — Organizing Dates

Before you can assemble a Review Board, you need to lock in a time that works for everyone involved.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a time that works for all parties">
    Reach out to the user under review, yourself, and a Leadership Team member to identify a time that fits everyone's availability. Do not set a date unilaterally — all three parties must be able to attend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Management Ticket">
    Once you have identified a suitable time, create a **Management Ticket** and add the member under review to it. Use this ticket to confirm the agreed-upon date and time with them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Form the Review Board around the confirmed time">
    After the date and time are confirmed, you can begin assembling your Review Board panel and scheduling all members around that window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2 — Assembling the Review Board

Before confirming any participants, you must verify each member with the Leadership Member who will be supervising the board. Your panel must include exactly **5 members** in the following composition:

- **Leadership Member** — x1
- **Management Member** — x1
- **Active Staff Member** — x1
- **Former Staff / Active Member** — x2

Once you have your five confirmed members, add them all to the ticket and ask whether any of them have objections to the staff or members involved. Panel members may request that a different person fill a role, **however, they cannot request a new Leadership or Management member** as a replacement.

<Warning>
  Every member selected for the Review Board must have **no relation to the case** and must not be a member currently under review. This includes personal relationships — panel members cannot be friends with those involved. If you are selecting an Active Staff Member, they **cannot be from Internal Affairs**. Additionally, any Manager, LT, Staff, Retired, or Active Member chosen must have **no prior history with or involvement in the original case**. Failure to observe this requirement compromises the integrity of the board.
</Warning>

## Step 3 — Conducting the Review

Once the date is confirmed and your panel is assembled, you are ready to run the Review Board. Make sure you are fully prepared before the session begins.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather your materials 10 minutes early">
    At least **10 minutes before** the scheduled start time, collect all of the following:

    - [The Review Board Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19v-iIGlave04KIXz4V3mvxElDjLt1C3JlOb9dsJrJMM/edit?usp=sharing)
    - [Review Board Form](https://forms.gle/HnJ2MrAKMiLg2kkR9)
    - A direct view of the user's Review Board infraction
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm everything is in order">
    Double-check that all three items above are accessible and accurate before proceeding. Do not begin the review if any materials are missing or unclear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Begin the review">
    Once you have confirmed that all information is in front of you and your panel is present, you are ready to begin the Review Board.
  </Step>
</Steps>
