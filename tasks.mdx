As a high-ranking team member at Networkerlc, you are expected to carry out a defined set of responsibilities tied to your role. Below you will find a comprehensive breakdown of all tasks and duties associated with each area of the management structure. These responsibilities are always applicable to your role, and additional tasks may be assigned as needed by senior leadership.

<Note>
  All tasks are assigned on a **weekly basis** by Directors or Senior Management and may vary week to week depending on current operations. Always check in with your assigned leadership contact at the start of each week to confirm your duties.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Weekly Tasks">
    All tasks assigned to management are distributed on a weekly cycle. These can range from Staff Supervisor Tasks to Discord Management Tasks, and are issued by either a Director or a Senior Management team member who oversees all Management and Staff Operations.

    **Your weekly task responsibilities include:**

    - Receive and review your weekly task assignments from a Director or Senior Management member
    - Complete all assigned Staff Supervisor Tasks within the designated week
    - Complete all assigned Discord Management Tasks within the designated week
    - Report progress or blockers to your overseeing Director or Senior Management contact
    - Ensure all tasks are fully resolved before the weekly cycle resets
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Community Management">
    As part of the Management Team, you are responsible for overseeing all aspects of the Networkerlc Discord server. Your role ensures the server runs smoothly, staff are properly supervised, and community concerns are addressed in a timely manner.

    **Your Community Management responsibilities include:**

    - Handle all Discord-related issues as they arise
    - Actively manage and support Discord staff members
    - Supervise Discord staff to ensure compliance with all applicable rules and guidelines
    - Manage and resolve incoming management tickets promptly
    - Maintain a healthy and well-organized Discord environment for the broader community
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Staff Supervision">
    The Staff Supervision role is responsible for overseeing all Staff Operations that may result in disciplinary action or punishment. You work in close coordination with the Management Team and directly oversee the Public Relations Team and Creative Department.

    **Your Staff Supervision responsibilities include:**

    - Work closely with the Management Team on any operational changes or updates
    - Issue weekly Quota Infractions to staff members who do not meet requirements
    - Handle and resolve management tickets in your area of oversight
    - Monitor Staff Members to ensure they are following all Staff Rules and Regulations
    - Oversee the day-to-day operations of the Public Relations Team
    - Oversee the day-to-day operations of the Creative Department
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  If you are ever unsure about your current tasks or responsibilities, don't hesitate to reach out to a Senior Management member or Director — they are there to guide you and ensure you're set up for success each week.
</Tip>
